# Citation and License Strategy

## GitHub

For each repository record owner/name, URL, commit or release, accessed date, detected license, relevant files, intended reuse and modifications. “No license” means copyright is reserved: link and summarize, but do not copy. Preserve attribution and notices for permitted reuse. Check file-level exceptions and dependency licenses.

## Papers and contest materials

Store title, authors, year, contest/problem/award, DOI or official URL, method tags, and original derived notes. Do not package full PDFs when paywalled, membership-only, or unclear. COMAP/UMAP content has specific copyright terms; link to it and abstract with credit. Award status is trusted only when official results or an identifiable award paper support it.

## Evidence grades

- S: official outstanding/first-prize paper, official commentary, or award paper with full code/data.
- A: finalist/meritorious paper, official problem commentary, peer-reviewed modeling-agent research.
- B: mature licensed implementation, strong course case, maintained code/tool.
- C: blog/forum/training material—lead generation only.

Derived notes must use original wording and cite the source. A manifest entry's `usage` field must say link-only, metadata/notes, evaluation data, or permitted adaptation.
