# Contest Compliance

Rules drift. Retrieve the current official notice at intake and again before submission. Record URL, publication/revision date, access time, and a rules checksum or archived copy when permitted.

## Intake checklist

- competition/year/problem; start/end/submission time and timezone;
- eligibility/team/advisor restrictions;
- allowed assistance, data, software and AI;
- required AI disclosure or usage log;
- language, anonymity, page/font/header/summary rules;
- required PDF/source/supporting files, names and size limits;
- problem-specific memo/letter/data deliverable;
- current official template and submission portal.

Current authoritative entrances include [COMAP instructions](https://www.contest.comap.com/undergraduate/contests/mcm/instructions.php) and the [CUMCM official site](https://www.mcm.edu.cn/). The CUMCM site published a 2026 revised paper-format notice and maintains a 2025 trial AI-tool rule; re-check the applicable year rather than hard-coding them.

## Submission gate

Fail closed on identity leakage, missing problem component, missing AI disclosure, unverified citations, page/file violation, non-reproducible headline result, stale summary, orphan figure/table, or unsupported claim. Record a human sign-off for content, numerics, references, rules, and final file hash.
